Vault is on the Mac now too: a native app, free like this one, with a vault of its own. Move items between them with an encrypted backup or by scanning QR codes.

- Backup passwords and items' own passwords are strengthened with Argon2id, so each guess at a leaked backup or a copied item costs far more.
- With an App Lock Password, the keys that check killphrases and search passphrases are kept inside the encrypted vault, so they go wherever it goes.
- Vault's widgets can no longer read the count of wrong App Lock Passwords.
