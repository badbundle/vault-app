Vault keeps private keys now, as well as recovery phrases: WIF and extended private keys (xprv, zprv and others), hex keys, Nostr nsec keys, and PGP, SSH and PEM private keys. Like a recovery phrase, a private key is always encrypted and locked, masked until you tap it, and never copied.

- Made for iPhone Duo: the lock screen lines up on the outer screen, and on the inner screen, partly folded, nothing sits across the crease.
- Recovery phrases and private keys now come first when you add an item, and Help has a new page about each.
- Vault runs with Apple's Enhanced Security, which makes memory bugs in it much harder to exploit.
